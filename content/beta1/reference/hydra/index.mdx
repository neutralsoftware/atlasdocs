`declare module "hydra"`

Atmosphere, weather, clouds, and water rendering.

## Classes

- [`WorleyNoise3D`](./worley-noise3-d) — Three-dimensional cellular noise with helpers producing texture handles.
- [`Clouds`](./clouds) — Volumetric cloud noise and rendering controls, including density, lighting steps, and wind.
- [`Atmosphere`](./atmosphere) — Day/night cycle, sky colors, celestial lighting, weather, and clouds.
- [`Fluid`](./fluid) — Renderable water surface with configurable extent, color, wave motion, and textures.

## Enums

- [`WeatherCondition`](./weather-condition) — Weather preset represented by a WeatherState.

## Types

- [`WeatherState`](./weather-state) — Weather condition, intensity, and wind returned by a weather delegate.
- [`WeatherDelegate`](./weather-delegate) — Computes weather from the current view and frame information.
