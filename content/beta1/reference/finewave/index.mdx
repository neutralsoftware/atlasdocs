`declare module "finewave"`

Audio playback, spatial listeners, and sound effects.

## Classes

- [`AudioEngine`](./audio-engine) — Audio device controls, master volume, and spatial listener state.
- [`AudioData`](./audio-data) — Audio data loaded from a resource for use by an AudioSource.
- [`AudioSource`](./audio-source) — Playback controls for audio data, including spatialization and effects.
- [`AudioEffect`](./audio-effect) — Base type for effects that can be applied to an AudioSource.
- [`Reverb`](./reverb) — Reverberation effect with room, damping, stereo width, and wet/dry controls.
- [`Echo`](./echo) — Delay effect with decay and wet/dry controls.
- [`Distortion`](./distortion) — Distortion effect with edge, gain, and low-pass filtering controls.
