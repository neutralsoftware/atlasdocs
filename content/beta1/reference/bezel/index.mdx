`declare module "bezel"`

Physics bodies, constraints, vehicles, and spatial query results.

## Types

- [`RaycastHit`](./raycast-hit) — Ray intersection information, including the hit object and surface normal.
- [`RaycastResult`](./raycast-result) — Ray query hits and nearest-hit information; hit is null when no closest hit exists.
- [`OverlapHit`](./overlap-hit) — Overlap contact information. penerationAxis retains the spelling used by this API.
- [`OverlapResult`](./overlap-result) — Overlap contacts and a flag indicating whether any were found.
- [`SweepHit`](./sweep-hit) — Contact encountered while sweeping a collider along a movement path.
- [`SweepResult`](./sweep-result) — Movement sweep contacts, nearest contact, and resulting end position.
- [`QueryResult`](./query-result) — Physics query payload. Inspect operation before reading the corresponding result field.
- [`WorldBody`](./world-body) — World attachment marker type used as a joint endpoint.
- [`JointMember`](./joint-member) — An object or world attachment used as one endpoint of a joint.
- [`Spring`](./spring) — Optional spring behavior with parameters interpreted according to mode.
- [`AngleLimits`](./angle-limits) — Optional minimum and maximum joint angles.
- [`Motor`](./motor) — Optional joint motor with force and torque limits.
- [`VehicleWheelSettings`](./vehicle-wheel-settings) — Wheel geometry, suspension, steering, and braking parameters.
- [`VehicleDifferential`](./vehicle-differential) — Torque distribution and differential settings for a pair of wheel indices.
- [`VehicleEngine`](./vehicle-engine) — Engine torque, RPM limits, inertia, and angular damping.
- [`VehicleTransmission`](./vehicle-transmission) — Forward/reverse gear ratios, clutch behavior, and shift timing.
- [`VehicleControllerSettings`](./vehicle-controller-settings) — Engine, transmission, and differential configuration for a vehicle.
- [`VehicleSettings`](./vehicle-settings) — Vehicle axes, wheel configurations, controller settings, and stability limits.
- [`CapsuleCollider`](./capsule-collider) — Capsule collision shape specified by radius and height.
- [`BoxCollider`](./box-collider) — Box collision shape specified by its three-dimensional size.
- [`SphereCollider`](./sphere-collider) — Sphere collision shape specified by radius.
- [`MeshCollider`](./mesh-collider) — Mesh collision shape descriptor.
- [`Collider`](./collider) — Supported collision shape descriptors for rigidbodies and physics queries.

## Enums

- [`QueryOperation`](./query-operation) — Identifies the physics query represented by a QueryResult.
- [`SpringMode`](./spring-mode) — Selects frequency/damping-ratio or stiffness/damping spring configuration.
- [`Space`](./space) — Coordinate space used for joint configuration.
- [`VehicleTransmissionMode`](./vehicle-transmission-mode) — Automatic or manual vehicle gear selection.

## Classes

- [`Joint`](./joint) — Base component for a physical constraint between two joint members.
- [`FixedJoint`](./fixed-joint) — Constraint that fixes the relative transform of its two members.
- [`HingeJoint`](./hinge-joint) — Constraint allowing rotation about a hinge axis, with optional limits and motor.
- [`SpringJoint`](./spring-joint) — Distance constraint with spring behavior and optional length limits.
- [`Vehicle`](./vehicle) — Physics vehicle component with driving inputs and configurable wheels and drivetrain.
- [`Rigidbody`](./rigidbody) — Physics component providing colliders, forces, velocities, tags, and spatial queries.
- [`Sensor`](./sensor) — Rigidbody initialized as a sensor for detecting contacts and sending signals.
