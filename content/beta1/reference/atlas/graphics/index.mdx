`declare module "atlas/graphics"`

Textures, render targets, post-processing, and lighting.

## Enums

- [`TextureType`](./texture-type) — Texture role used by the renderer, such as albedo, normal, depth, or material data.
- [`RenderTargetType`](./render-target-type) — Framebuffer purpose and attachment configuration.
- [`RenderPassType`](./render-pass-type) — Rendering pipeline pass to enqueue for a render target.

## Classes

- [`Texture`](./texture) — GPU texture loaded from a resource or created procedurally.
- [`Cubemap`](./cubemap) — Cube texture assembled from face resources, with helpers for updating face colors.
- [`RenderTarget`](./render-target) — Offscreen rendering destination with output textures, render passes, and post-processing effects.
- [`Skybox`](./skybox) — Scene background rendered from a cubemap.
- [`AmbientLight`](./ambient-light) — Uniform ambient illumination described by color and intensity.
- [`Light`](./light) — Point light with position, range, diffuse color, and specular shine color.
- [`DirectionalLight`](./directional-light) — Light with a direction and no positional origin, suitable for sunlight.
- [`SpotLight`](./spot-light) — Positioned cone light with inner and outer cutoffs, direction, and range.
- [`AreaLight`](./area-light) — Oriented area light with dimensions, emission range, and optional two-sided emission.

## Values

- [`Effects`](./effects) — Built-in post-processing effect descriptors. Copy a descriptor to customize its parameters.

## Types

- [`Effect`](./effect) — A built-in effect name or an effect descriptor containing its parameters.
